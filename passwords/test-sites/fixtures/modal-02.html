The pencil, reconsidered

It began, as these things do, with a shortage. By the spring the workshop had run out of cedar and the foreman had run out of patience.

What followed was a decade of experiments in incense wood, recycled newsprint and, briefly, compressed sawdust from a nearby furniture factory.
